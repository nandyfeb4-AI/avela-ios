# Product Vision

## Mission

Help people build healthier habits around attention and information consumption without guilt, rigid streak mechanics, or excessive tracking.

## Target user

Primary user:
- iPhone user
- cares about productivity, habits, focus, or digital wellbeing
- feels distracted by news, social media, video, or doomscrolling
- wants awareness and self-regulation rather than aggressive blocking

Secondary user:
- already uses a habit tracker but finds it rigid, noisy, guilt-inducing, or too maintenance-heavy

## Core promise

Build better habits and regain control of your attention.

## Product positioning

A premium native iOS habit and attention-management system that combines:

- flexible habit tracking
- attention budgets
- recovery-oriented progress
- behavioral insight
- a calm adaptive companion

## Differentiation

The product should be distinguishable from generic habit trackers through real behavior, not App Store copy alone.

Primary differentiators:
1. Attention / information budgets
2. Flexible weekly commitments
3. Recovery after misses
4. Weekly behavioral insight
5. Spirit-animal companion as a stateful interface layer
6. Native iOS experience across app, widgets, notifications, and Live Activities

## Long-term direction

Habit tracker
→ attention-management system
→ personal behavioral intelligence layer

## Product principles

- Calm, not punitive
- Helpful, not preachy
- Flexible, not rigid
- Insightful, not data-heavy
- Private by default
- Native iOS first
- No feature exists only to look impressive
- No medical or therapeutic claims

## Non-goals

V1 is not:
- a social network
- a therapy or medical app
- a child-control / parental-control app
- a rewards-heavy RPG
- a cross-platform app
- an AI chatbot
- an enterprise productivity tool
