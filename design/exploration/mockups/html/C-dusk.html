Assessment

  1. The best-suited direction for the future companion, widgets and Live Activity glance (a lit creature reads well on the Lock Screen).
  2. Risk: rings and gems push toward the visual language of Finch and fitness rings; it is less differentiated from Streaks’ grid of circles.
  3. Risk: the light mode is weak, and the stage costs about 250pt of the first screen, pushing the next action below the fold with 6+ habits.
  4. Risk: highest motion and transparency budget, so the most Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency work.
  5. Recommended to adopt later: the stage pattern for the companion detail sheet, and the halo-as-state idea for widgets.
